What you get
  • A new answer to the price objection — "part of this is pre-tax" — at full fee

  • Compliance handled. Caps, primary-care-only rules, and the sole-fee test are checked before anything bills
What you don't deal with
  • No new merchant account. You stay the merchant of record on your own Stripe account, set up as a medical practice
